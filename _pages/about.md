I am a Staff Research Engineer at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/), working on the Frontier Safety Mitigation in San Francisco. My research focuses on designing safeguards against cybersecurity threats and biological risks. You can find more about our work [here](https://deepmind.google/blog/taking-a-responsible-path-to-agi/).

Previously, I contributed to evaluating LLM's stealth and situational awareness capability [research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01420) and improving Gemini's coding capabilities through pre- and post-training. Prior to my career in AI, I worked in algorithmic trading in [BNP Paribas](https://group.bnpparibas/en/).

I believe the principles of [Effective Altruism](https://80000hours.org/2020/08/misconceptions-effective-altruism/) and have pledged a portion of my income to [high-impact charities](https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/best-charities-to-donate-to-2026).

In my free time, you can find me rock climbing, hiking, traveling, or writing for my [blog](https://ziyuewang.substack.com/).
